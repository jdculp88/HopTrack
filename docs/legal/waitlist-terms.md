## What You're Signing Up For

By submitting your email address on the HopTrack waitlist ("Notify Me" signup), you are requesting to be notified when HopTrack launches or when early access becomes available.

This is not a purchase, subscription, or binding commitment. You are joining a mailing list.

---

## What We Collect

When you sign up for the waitlist, we collect:

- **Email address** — to notify you about launch updates and early access
- **Timestamp** — when you signed up
- **Signup type** — whether you signed up as a beer drinker, brewery owner, or both
- **Referral source** — how you found us (if applicable)

We do **not** collect payment information, location data, or any other personal information during waitlist signup.

---

## How We Use Your Email

We will use your email address to:

1. Notify you when HopTrack launches or early access is available
2. Send occasional pre-launch updates (no more than 2x per month)
3. Offer early access or beta testing opportunities

We will **not**:
- Sell your email address to third parties
- Share your email with brewery partners or advertisers
- Send spam or unrelated marketing

---

## Email Service

Waitlist emails are sent via **Resend** (our email service provider). Your email address is stored in our Supabase database (hosted on AWS in the US).

---

## Unsubscribe

Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. You can remove yourself from the waitlist at any time by:

- Clicking the unsubscribe link in any email
- Emailing privacy@hoptrack.beer with the subject "Unsubscribe"

We will process unsubscribe requests within 3 business days (typically instant).

## Age Requirement

HopTrack is a craft beer platform. **You must be at least 21 years old** to sign up for the waitlist. By submitting your email, you confirm that you are 21 or older.

## CAN-SPAM Compliance

All emails from HopTrack comply with the CAN-SPAM Act:

- Every email identifies [HopTrack LLC] as the sender

- Subject lines accurately reflect email content
- We do not use deceptive headers or misleading subject lines
