## 4. Auto-Renewal

**YOUR SUBSCRIPTION WILL AUTOMATICALLY RENEW** at the end of each billing period (monthly or annually) at the then-current price, unless you cancel before the renewal date.

- **Monthly subscriptions:** Renew on the same date each month
- **Annual subscriptions:** Renew on the anniversary of your start date
- We will send a renewal reminder email at least **7 days before** annual renewals and at least **3 days before** monthly renewals
- You may cancel auto-renewal at any time through your billing settings or by contacting billing@hoptrack.beer
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period — you are not charged again after cancellation

### Clear and Conspicuous Disclosure (FTC Negative Option Rule)

Before you are charged for the first time, we will clearly disclose:
1. That your subscription will automatically renew and you will be charged on a recurring basis
2. The amount you will be charged and the frequency of charges
3. How to cancel your subscription before being charged

You must provide **affirmative consent** (checking a box, clicking a clearly labeled "Subscribe" button) before any recurring charges begin. Pre-checked boxes do not constitute valid consent.

We provide a **simple cancellation mechanism** — you can cancel through your billing settings in the app at any time without having to contact support, call a phone number, or navigate unnecessary retention flows. Email cancellation (billing@hoptrack.beer) is also available as an alternative.

This disclosure is made in compliance with:
- **FTC Act Section 5 + ROSCA** (Restoring Online Shoppers' Confidence Act) — requires clear disclosure, affirmative consent, and simple cancellation for all negative option offers. Note: The FTC's 2024 amended Negative Option Rule (16 CFR Part 425) was vacated by the Eighth Circuit on July 8, 2025, but the underlying ROSCA and FTC Act obligations remain fully enforceable.
- **California Automatic Renewal Law (CARL)** (Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 17600 et seq., as amended July 1, 2025) — requires express affirmative consent, online cancellation if signup was online, a "prominently located direct link or button" for cancellation, renewal reminder 15-45 days before annual renewals, and if save/discount offers are shown during cancellation a simultaneous "click to cancel" button must be visible. **Consent records must be maintained for 3 years or 1 year after termination (whichever is longer).**
- **New York General Business Law § 527-a** (as amended November 5, 2025) — requires clear disclosure, written confirmation, and for price increases: advance consent OR 14-day penalty-free cancellation with prorated refund
- **Other applicable state auto-renewal laws** including those in Arkansas, Colorado, Connecticut, Illinois, Maryland, Massachusetts, Minnesota, Oregon, Utah, and Virginia

**By subscribing, you affirmatively consent to automatic renewal and recurring charges until you cancel.**

## 5. Cancellation

### How to Cancel
- **Self-service:** Go to your brewery dashboard → Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription
- **Email:** Send a cancellation request to billing@hoptrack.beer
- **Effective immediately:** Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You retain access to paid features until then.

### What Happens When You Cancel
- Your brewery profile and public listing remain active
- Your tap list, beer data, and historical analytics are preserved (read-only)
- Dashboard management features (editing tap list, loyalty programs, promotions) are disabled
- Customer data you accessed through HopTrack is no longer accessible via the dashboard
- You can resubscribe at any time to regain full access

### We reserve the right to cancel subscriptions for:
- Violation of our Terms of Service
- Fraud or abuse
- Non-payment (after the grace period described above)
