## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and understand how you use the service. Some cookies are essential for the site to work; others help us improve your experience.

## How HopTrack Uses Cookies

### Essential Cookies (Required)

These cookies are necessary for HopTrack to function. They cannot be disabled.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | Set By |
|--------|---------|----------|--------|
| `sb-access-token` | Supabase authentication session | Session | Supabase |
| `sb-refresh-token` | Supabase session refresh | 7 days | Supabase |
| `cookie-consent` | Records your cookie preferences | 1 year | HopTrack |
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Language/locale preference | 1 year | Next.js |

### Functional Cookies (Optional)

These cookies improve your experience but are not required.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | Set By |
|--------|---------|----------|--------|
| `theme` | Dark/light mode preference | 1 year | HopTrack |
| `sidebar-collapsed` | UI layout preference | 1 year | HopTrack |

### Analytics Cookies (Optional)

We may use analytics to understand how people use HopTrack. Currently, we use **Sentry** for error tracking only (not behavioral analytics). If we add analytics tools in the future, this section will be updated.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | Set By |
|--------|---------|----------|--------|
| Sentry session replay (if enabled) | Error reproduction | Session | Sentry |

### Third-Party Cookies

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. We do not allow third-party advertising networks to place cookies on HopTrack.

Stripe may set cookies when you interact with payment forms (brewery billing only). These are governed by Stripe's cookie policy.

## Your Cookie Choices

When you first visit HopTrack, a cookie consent banner gives you the choice to accept or decline non-essential cookies.

You can also manage cookies through:

- **Browser settings:** Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Note that blocking essential cookies will prevent HopTrack from working properly.
- **In-app:** Cookie preferences are stored locally and can be reset by clearing your browser data.

### How to Manage Cookies in Your Browser

- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
- **Firefox:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari:** Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
- **Edge:** Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions

## Do Not Track

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. There is no industry-standard response to DNT. HopTrack does not currently respond to DNT signals because we do not engage in cross-site tracking.
